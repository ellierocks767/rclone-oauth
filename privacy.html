Privacy policy

This OAuth client belongs to a single person and is used only with their own Google account, through rclone running on their own computer.

  • Google Drive data is accessed only to upload, list, download and delete the account owner's own files.
  • No data is collected, stored on any server, sold or shared with anyone. The OAuth token stays on the owner's computer.
  • No other users are served. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
