rclone (personal)

This Google OAuth client is used by one person, Ellie, with rclone on her own computer. It backs up her files to her own Google Drive and lets her browse that Drive in her file manager.

It is not a public service: no one else signs in with it, and nothing is sent anywhere other than Ellie's own Google Drive. See the privacy policy and terms.
